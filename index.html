01 · Affiliate marketing basics

Affiliate marketing is a performance-based arrangement. You share a tracked link to a product or service. If someone follows that link and completes an eligible action under the affiliate programme's rules, the programme may attribute a commission to you.

Know these terms

  • Affiliate link: a unique tracking URL associated with your account or campaign.
  • Attribution: the programme's method for deciding which referral receives credit.
  • Qualifying purchase: an order that meets the programme's conditions, after cancellations, returns and exclusions are considered.
  • Commission: the amount approved under the programme's rate and rules.

Never promise earnings. A click alone usually does not mean a commission is earned. Disclose that a link may earn you a commission, and do not mislead people about prices, discounts or product quality.

Important: Affiliate commissions are not guaranteed. Rates, eligibility, attribution windows, approval, payout thresholds and account-age rules can change. Always check the current official terms before joining or sharing links.

About the platform

What is Shopper's Suggestions?

A product-discovery marketplace designed to help people find useful recommendations and connect with promoters who share them.

Not a Shopify store or a conventional dropshipping store

Shopper's Suggestions is built around discovering products and sharing recommendations. It is not presented as a store that owns inventory or fulfils orders. Product purchases are completed through the relevant retailer or merchant.

For people discovering products

Visitors can browse product suggestions, explore categories, follow promoters and save products they want to revisit. The goal is to make useful discoveries easier to find.

For promoters

Promoters share products with their audience using eligible affiliate links. They can build a profile and publish recommendations through the platform, subject to its current account and posting rules.

Telegram integration

The Telegram bot can provide an entry point to the community and share product updates or promotional messages. Product announcements may include available product media and relevant links. Always check the message and destination before sharing.

How promotional messages work

Promotional posts are a way to bring product discoveries to a Telegram audience. They should be clearly identifiable as promotional, describe products accurately and avoid spam. A post or click does not guarantee a sale or commission.

Our purpose

Help people discover useful products while giving promoters a place to organise and share recommendations transparently.
